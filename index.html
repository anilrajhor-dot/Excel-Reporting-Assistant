How every answer is built

  1. Clarify the data. The Your Data tab confirms heading row, first column and column types before any formula is written.
  2. Exact solution. You get the formula with real cell references, one plain sentence on what it does, and numbered menu steps.
  3. Right format. Each answer says whether a formula, a PivotTable or a chart fits best, and which chart type.
  4. Usable. A worked example from your own rows and a list of common mistakes come with it.
  5. Beginner-friendly. No jargon, and a simpler alternative where one exists. Choose Advanced above to collapse the step lists.
